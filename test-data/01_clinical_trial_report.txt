PRIMARY EFFICACY RESULTS
================================================================================

PRIMARY ENDPOINT: HbA1c reduction ≥ 1.5% at week 52

  Arm           N    Responders  Response Rate  Mean HbA1c Drop  p-value
  ----------    ---  ----------  -------------  ---------------  -------
  Velarixan     301  211         70.1%          -2.31%           <0.001
  Placebo       298  47          15.8%          -0.44%           (ref)

  Number Needed to Treat (NNT): 1.85
  Absolute Risk Reduction: 54.3%
  Relative Risk Reduction: 71.5%

SECONDARY ENDPOINTS:
  - Fasting glucose reduction: -44.2 mg/dL (Velarixan) vs -6.1 mg/dL (Placebo)
  - Weight reduction: -3.8 kg (Velarixan) vs +0.4 kg (Placebo)
  - Systolic BP reduction: -8.2 mmHg (Velarixan) vs -1.1 mmHg (Placebo)

SERIOUS ADVERSE EVENTS:
  - Pancreatitis: 3 cases (Velarixan arm) — causal relationship under review
  - Hypoglycemia grade 3+: 7 cases (Velarixan) vs 1 case (Placebo)
  - Death: 1 (MC-005, Placebo arm — cardiac event, unrelated per DSMB review)
