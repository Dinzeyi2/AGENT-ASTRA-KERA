================================================================================
GENOMIC SUMMARY STATISTICS
================================================================================

  High-risk PRS (>0.80):       23.4% of cohort
  APOE e4/e4 carriers:          4.2% of cohort
  BRCA1 pathogenic variants:    1.8% of cohort
  MACE incidence (8yr):        18.7% overall | 44.1% in high-PRS quartile
  All-cause mortality:          6.2% overall | 14.8% in high-PRS + APOE e4/e4

KEY FINDING:
  Participants with PRS_CVD > 0.85 AND APOE e4/e4 genotype had a 14.2x
  increased risk of MACE vs the reference group (HR 14.2, 95% CI 8.8-22.9).
  This interaction effect was not captured by traditional Framingham scoring.

PROPOSED CLINICAL APPLICATION:
  Integration of PRS_CVD into routine screening for patients aged 45-75
  could identify 31% of future MACE cases 5-10 years before clinical onset.
